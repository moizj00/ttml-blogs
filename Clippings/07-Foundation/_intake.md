# Foundation Intake — Filling Slots 04–10

The empty slots are the most valuable in the whole vault. Slots 04–07 are **your own best pieces** — that's where the agent learns *your* real voice, not a generic one.

## To add one of your own pieces (full text + notes)
1. Paste the article text into chat, or drop it as a `.md` file in `01-Voice/Samples/` (or anywhere in the vault).
2. Say: **"Add this to Foundation-10 as entry 04"** (or 05, 06...).
3. The agent will:
   - Create the entry from [[_TEMPLATE]] with your **full text**.
   - Write the breakdown (hook, structure, voice, moves).
   - Note what's distinctly *yours* and fold it into [[voice-fingerprint]].

## To add a famous piece you admire (slots 08–09)
1. Send the **link** (and a line on why you love it).
2. The agent stores the link + a deep breakdown — not the full copyrighted text.

## What makes a good pick
- A post you were proud of, or one that **performed** (ranked, converted, got shared).
- Variety helps: one explainer, one persuasive piece, one with personality.
- Don't pre-edit. Imperfections are voice signal.

## Tip
Each piece you add makes the next blog draft sound more like you. Four solid own-pieces is enough to shift the agent's default voice noticeably; more is better.
