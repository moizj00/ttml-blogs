# Catalog: Narrative & Essays With Soul (~18)

Where "almost human-like sentiment" is the whole point. Even legal blog posts get their warmth from these moves — show, don't tell; restraint over melodrama; one specific detail that holds the feeling.

## Canonical literary nonfiction / essays
1. ⭐ **Joan Didion — Goodbye to All That** 🔎 — *Personal disillusionment via concrete New York detail. Show the city, feel the heartbreak.*
2. **Joan Didion — The White Album** ("We tell ourselves stories in order to live") 🔎 — *Opening line as thesis and music at once.*
3. ⭐ **George Orwell — Shooting an Elephant** — https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/shooting-an-elephant/ ✅ — *A single event carries a whole argument about power. Scene as essay.*
4. **George Orwell — A Hanging** — orwellfoundation.com 🔎 — *The puddle-stepping detail does the moral work. Show, don't preach.*
5. ⭐ **E.B. White — Once More to the Lake** 🔎 — *Time and mortality through sensory repetition. The last line detonates.*
6. **David Foster Wallace — Consider the Lobster** 🔎 — *Turn a food festival into ethics; footnotes as voice.*
7. ⭐ **David Foster Wallace — This Is Water** (Kenyon speech) — widely archived 🔎 — *Plain parable + direct address = unforgettable. The empathy move.*
8. **James Baldwin — Notes of a Native Son** 🔎 — *Personal grief braided with public truth; controlled fury.*
9. **Annie Dillard — Pilgrim at Tinker Creek / The Writing Life** 🔎 — *Attention as devotion; the precise image over the abstract claim.*
10. **Gay Talese — Frank Sinatra Has a Cold** (Esquire) 🔎 — *Profile without the interview; detail and scene build the man.*
11. **Tom Wolfe — The Right Stuff / essays** 🔎 — *Energy and rhythm; punctuation as performance (use sparingly).*
12. ⭐ **Nora Ephron — A Few Words About Breasts / I Feel Bad About My Neck** 🔎 — *Funny + vulnerable + specific. Humor as honesty.*

## Story craft references
13. **Kurt Vonnegut — How to Write With Style** (essay) 🔎 — *"Find a subject you care about." Care is audible.*
14. **Anne Lamott — Bird by Bird** ("Shitty First Drafts") 🔎 — *Permission to be bad first; the draft is for discovery.*
15. **Ira Glass — on the Taste Gap** (interview, widely transcribed) 🔎 — *Why beginners quit, and why volume closes the gap.*
16. **Pixar's 22 Rules of Storytelling** — widely archived 🔎 — *Structure beats inspiration; "what's the essential?"*
17. ⭐ **Ted Chiang — Story of Your Life / essays** 🔎 — *Restraint and idea-density; emotion earned through structure.*
18. **George Saunders — A Swim in a Pond in the Rain** 🔎 — *Line-by-line on why a sentence works. The close-reading masterclass.*

## How even a legal post borrows from these
- Open a TTML post with a one-sentence **scene** ("The notice was taped to her door on a Tuesday.") before the legal answer — then the reader is *in it*.
- Name the feeling by **showing** it (the unopened envelope, the unanswered email), per [[creative-narrative]] and [[core-principles]] #7.
- Understate. The calm, plain sentence reassures a scared reader more than reassurance does.
