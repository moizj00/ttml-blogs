# Catalog: Persuasion, Copywriting & Sales (~18)

How to move a reader to act without sounding like a salesman. Directly relevant to TTML CTAs and landing pages.

## The legends (study the moves, not the era)
1. ⭐ **David Ogilvy — Rolls-Royce ad** ("At 60 miles an hour the loudest noise...") 🔎 — *One ultra-specific detail sells luxury better than ten adjectives. Concrete > superlative.*
2. ⭐ **David Ogilvy — Confessions of an Advertising Man** (book) 🔎 — *"The consumer isn't a moron; she's your wife." Respect the reader.*
3. **Ogilvy — headline principle** 🔎 — *5x more people read the headline than the body. Spend 80% of effort there.*
4. ⭐ **Gary Halbert — The Coat of Arms Letter** 🔎 — *Most-mailed letter in history. Hyper-relevant personalization + curiosity.*
5. **Gary Halbert — "Desperate Nerd From Ohio"** 🔎 — *Conversational, story-first, raw. People buy from people.*
6. **Gary Halbert — The Boron Letters** — widely archived 🔎 — *Teaches the mindset: AIDA, research, and writing to one person.*
7. ⭐ **Eugene Schwartz — Breakthrough Advertising** (book) 🔎 — *Meet the reader at their stage of awareness. Don't sell water to the drowning.*
8. **Claude Hopkins — Scientific Advertising** — public domain, e.g. https://www.gutenberg.org/ 🔎 — *Specificity and testing. "Specific claims beat generalities."*
9. **Robert Collier — The Robert Collier Letter Book** 🔎 — *"Enter the conversation already in the reader's head."*
10. **Joe Sugarman — The Adweek Copywriting Handbook** 🔎 — *Every sentence's job is to get the next one read. The "slippery slide."*

## Modern persuasion & frameworks
11. ⭐ **Robert Cialdini — Influence** (6 principles) 🔎 — *Reciprocity, scarcity, authority, consistency, liking, social proof. The grammar of persuasion.*
12. **Chip & Dan Heath — Made to Stick** (SUCCESs) 🔎 — *Simple, Unexpected, Concrete, Credible, Emotional, Stories. A stickiness checklist.*
13. **Donald Miller — StoryBrand** 🔎 — *Make the customer the hero, the brand the guide. Reframes every CTA.*
14. **Copyhackers (copyhackers.com)** — https://copyhackers.com/blog ✅ — *Conversion copy backed by voice-of-customer research. Steal their teardown format.*
15. **Basecamp / 37signals copy** 🔎 — *Plain, confident, anti-hype product writing. The TTML tone target for sales pages.*
16. **Stripe's product & docs writing** — https://stripe.com ✅ — *Clarity as a competitive advantage; precise verbs, zero fluff.*
17. **Apple product page copy** 🔎 — *Benefit-first lines, generous whitespace, one idea per screen.*
18. ⭐ **Bencivenga's "Marketing Bullets"** 🔎 — *The bullet as a curiosity engine; promise a payoff, withhold just enough.*

## How TTML should apply persuasion
- Sell the **outcome** (the letter that ends the dispute), not the feature (a PDF).
- Use **one** specific proof — a real result, number, or example — over any superlative.
- The CTA is **earned by the free help above it.** See [[03-legal-explainer-pattern]].
- Stay on the right side of [[anti-ai-tells]]: persuasion, not hype. Confidence reads as trust; hype reads as desperation.
