# Catalog: Essay & Writing Craft (~30)

The school of clear, human, persuasive prose. The backbone of the TTML voice.

## Paul Graham (paulgraham.com) — the core
Essay index: https://paulgraham.com/articles.html ✅ (every link below lives off this index)

1. ⭐ **Write Like You Talk** — https://paulgraham.com/talk.html ✅ — *Read it aloud; if you wouldn't say it, cut it.* (See [[01-pg-write-like-you-talk]])
2. ⭐ **Writing, Briefly** — https://paulgraham.com/writing44.html ✅ — *Draft ugly, then delete relentlessly.* (See [[02-pg-writing-briefly]])
3. **The Age of the Essay** — https://paulgraham.com/essay.html ✅ — *An essay is following a question, not defending a thesis. Start with a real question.*
4. **Write Simply** — https://paulgraham.com/simply.html ✅ — *Simple writing = honest writing. Complexity often hides weak thinking.*
5. **How to Write Usefully** — https://paulgraham.com/useful.html ✅ — *Useful = important × correct × novel × strength. A checklist for worth.*
6. **Putting Ideas Into Words** — https://paulgraham.com/words.html ✅ — *Writing is how you find out what you actually think. Don't outline to death.*
7. **The Best Essay** — https://paulgraham.com/best.html ✅ — *Surprise the reader; chase the thing you didn't expect to find.*
8. **Good Writing** — https://paulgraham.com/goodwriting.html 🔎 — *Good writing is rewriting; the first version is just raw material.*

## Morgan Housel (collaborativefund.com / morganhousel.com)
9. ⭐ **The Psychology of Money** (essay, pre-book) 🔎 — *Lead with a counterintuitive human truth, prove it with one vivid story.*
10. **How to Read Financial News** / **Getting Rich vs. Staying Rich** 🔎 — *One idea, ruthless concision, a memorable last line.*
11. **Save Like a Pessimist, Invest Like an Optimist** 🔎 — *Title carries the whole argument as a paradox.*

## Patrick McKenzie ("patio11", kalzumeus.com / bitsaboutmoney.com)
12. ⭐ **Salary Negotiation** — https://www.kalzumeus.com/2012/01/23/salary-negotiation/ 🔎 — *Exhaustive, generous, reader-first; teach so well the CTA is unnecessary.*
13. **Falsehoods Programmers Believe About Names** 🔎 — *The list-as-argument: a barrage of specifics that demolishes a lazy assumption.*

## Derek Sivers (sive.rs)
14. **Sivers' essays index** — https://sive.rs/blog ✅ — *Radical brevity. Most posts are under 300 words and land harder for it.*
15. ⭐ **How to Tell If You're Doing It Right / "Hell Yeah or No"** 🔎 — *A single decision rule, named memorably, explained in a breath.*

## Seth Godin (seths.blog)
16. **Seth's blog** — https://seths.blog ✅ — *Daily proof that one small idea, sharply framed, beats a long post.*
17. ⭐ **"The dip" / "This might not work"** 🔎 — *Permission + provocation in a few lines. Title does heavy lifting.*

## Classic / canonical on writing
18. ⭐ **George Orwell — Politics and the English Language** — https://www.orwellfoundation.com/the-orwell-foundation/orwell/essays-and-other-works/politics-and-the-english-language/ ✅ — *Six rules; the original anti-jargon manifesto. Required.*
19. **William Zinsser — On Writing Well** (book; clutter chapter excerpts widely available) 🔎 — *Cut clutter; every word must earn its place.*
20. **Strunk & White — The Elements of Style** — https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/37134 ✅ — *"Omit needless words." The foundation under all the above.*
21. **Stephen King — On Writing** (memoir/craft) 🔎 — *"The road to hell is paved with adverbs." Kill the -ly.*
22. **Verlyn Klinkenborg — Several Short Sentences About Writing** 🔎 — *The sentence is the unit. Make each one strong and independent.*

## Modern voices worth studying
23. **Tim Urban — Wait But Why** — https://waitbutwhy.com ✅ — *Make hard ideas addictive with structure, stakes, and plain analogies.*
24. ⭐ **Tim Urban — Why Procrastinators Procrastinate** — https://waitbutwhy.com/2013/10/why-procrastinators-procrastinate.html ✅ — *Name an internal experience precisely and the reader feels seen.*
25. **Ben Thompson — Stratechery** — https://stratechery.com ✅ — *Build an argument brick by brick; define terms, then reason forward.*
26. **Paul Ford — What Is Code?** (Bloomberg) 🔎 — *Voice + scope: a huge topic made warm and human.*
27. **James Clear — Atomic Habits essays** — https://jamesclear.com/articles ✅ — *One principle per piece, a concrete protocol, a sticky name.*
28. **Maria Popova — The Marginalian** — https://www.themarginalian.org ✅ — *Synthesis as art; braid quotes and ideas into something new.*
29. **rauchg / dev essays** 🔎 — *Strong technical writing: claim, evidence, implication, no filler.*
30. **Slate Star Codex / Astral Codex Ten — Scott Alexander** — https://www.astralcodexten.com ✅ — *Steelman the other side harder than they do, then respond.*

> Move that recurs across all 30: **plain words, one idea, surprise, and a last line that turns instead of summarizes.**
