# Catalog: Legal / TTML-Relevant Explainers (~22)

The direct competitive set and quality bar for TTML blog content. Study how the best legal sites stay plain, useful, and trustworthy — then beat them on voice and warmth.

## Nolo (nolo.com) — the gold standard for plain-English law
Encyclopedia hub: https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia ✅

1. ⭐ **How to Write a Winning Personal Injury Demand Letter** — https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/write-winning-demand-letter-29858.html 🔎 — *Facts → liability → damages → deadline. The demand-letter skeleton.*
2. **How to Write a Settlement Demand Letter** — https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/free-books/small-claims-book/chapter6-4.html 🔎 — *Businesslike tone beats emotional rhetoric; structure signals seriousness.*
3. **What to Do When You Receive a Demand Letter** — https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/what-to-do-when-you-receive-a-demand-letter.html 🔎 — *Answer the panicked searcher's exact question first.*
4. ⭐ **Contracts 101** — https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/contracts-101 🔎 — *Define one term plainly (offer, consideration) before building.*
5. **Renters' & Tenants' Rights** — https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/renters-rights 🔎 — *Topic clusters: pets, deposits, repairs, privacy — each its own clean answer.*
6. **State Landlord-Tenant Laws** — https://www.nolo.com/legal-encyclopedia/state-landlord-tenant-laws 🔎 — *State-by-state tables = the localization play TTML should copy for CA.*
7. **How to File and Win a Small Claims Case** 🔎 — *Numbered, doable steps. Earns the right to a list.*
8. **Security Deposit Limits & Deadlines (by state)** 🔎 — *Specific numbers and dates build authority faster than adjectives.*

## Other strong plain-language legal sources
12. **Avvo / legal Q&A explainers** 🔎 — *Real-question framing pulled straight from search intent.*
13. **LegalZoom learning center** — https://www.legalzoom.com/articles ✅ — *Soft, earned CTA after genuinely helping — the model for TTML's close.*
14. **Rocket Lawyer legal guides** — https://www.rocketlawyer.com/legal-guides ✅ — *Template + explainer combo; show the artifact, not just the theory.*
15. **California Courts Self-Help (selfhelp.courts.ca.gov)** — https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov ✅ — *Authoritative CA-specific procedure; cite for trust, then humanize.*
16. **DoNotPay-style "how to fight X" explainers** 🔎 — *Confident, action-first, slightly irreverent — voice lesson for standing out.*
17. **Nolo — Statute of Limitations explainers** 🔎 — *Name the deadline risk early; urgency that's honest, not manufactured.*
18. **Demand letter guides (justicedirect, etc.)** — https://justicedirect.com/post/demand-letter 🔎 — *One measurable demand + exact deadline date. Concrete > vague.*
19. **Cornell LII (law.cornell.edu)** — https://www.law.cornell.edu ✅ — *Primary-source backbone; link to the statute, then translate it.*
20. ** Tenant union / legal-aid explainers** 🔎 — *Warmth + advocacy voice; writes for the scared person, not the court.*
21. **Consumer Financial Protection Bureau — Ask CFPB** — https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/ ✅ — *Government plain-language masterclass: short question, short answer.*
22. **Plain Language legal writing (CBA)** — https://www.cba.org/resources/cba-practicelink/ 🔎 — *The profession's own case for dropping jargon. Ammunition for our style.*

## The TTML differentiator (what none of these do well)
Most legal sites are correct but cold. TTML's edge: the same accuracy, written like [[01-pg-write-like-you-talk|a human talking to a worried friend]], with the [[03-legal-explainer-pattern|answer-first → name the fear → earned CTA]] structure. Study these for *what to cover and how to rank*; beat them on *how it feels to read.*
