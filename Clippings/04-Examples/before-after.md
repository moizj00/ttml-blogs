# Before / After — The Rules Made Visible

Concrete rewrites. When a rule feels abstract, look here. Each pair shows the AI-default version and the human version.

---

## 1. Essay opening
**Before (AI default):**
> In today's fast-paced and ever-evolving world, productivity has become more important than ever. In this article, we'll explore several key strategies that can help you unlock your full potential.

**After:**
> I wasted four years optimizing a system that was solving the wrong problem. Here's the part nobody tells you: doing more, faster, is how you lose.

*Why:* killed the throat-clearing and the word-cloud ("ever-evolving," "unlock," "potential"), opened with a concrete confession and a stake.

---

## 2. Announcing emotion → showing it
**Before:**
> Losing my grandfather was an incredibly difficult and deeply emotional experience that affected me profoundly.

**After:**
> For a month after, I kept his number in my phone. I'd scroll past it on purpose.

*Why:* the detail does the grief. No emotion-labels, no intensifiers.

---

## 3. Corporate email
**Before:**
> I hope this email finds you well. I wanted to reach out to touch base regarding the project and see if we could potentially circle back on the deliverables at your earliest convenience.

**After:**
> Quick one — can you send the final deliverables by Thursday? We need them to hit the launch date.

*Why:* BLUF, plain English, one clear ask, a real deadline.

---

## 4. The fake antithesis
**Before:**
> This isn't just a tool. It's a movement.

**After:**
> It started as a weekend script. Forty thousand people use it now, and I still don't fully understand why.

*Why:* replaced the tired "not just X, it's Y" with a specific, slightly vulnerable claim.

---

## 5. Social hook
**Before:**
> Here are some thoughts on why consistency is important for success. A thread 🧵👇

**After:**
> I posted every day for a year. 300 posts did nothing. 3 changed my life. Here's what the 3 had in common.

*Why:* specific numbers, a curiosity gap, no filler announcement.

---

## 6. Hedging → conviction
**Before:**
> It could perhaps be argued that, in many cases, remote work tends to be somewhat more productive for certain types of roles.

**After:**
> Remote work makes focused roles more productive. It makes collaborative ones harder. Pretending it's all upside is how teams break.

*Why:* cut the hedge-stack, took a real position, acknowledged the tradeoff honestly.

---

## 7. Adjective stack → strong noun + verb
**Before:**
> The beautiful, sprawling, magnificent old house stood impressively on the hill.

**After:**
> The house dominated the hill — three stories of peeling Victorian that the town had stopped trying to fix.

*Why:* one precise image beats four decorative adjectives.

---

## 8. Marketing copy
**Before:**
> Our cutting-edge, best-in-class platform leverages robust AI to deliver seamless, game-changing results.

**After:**
> It drafts your first version in about ten seconds. Most people edit instead of starting from a blank page. That's the whole pitch.

*Why:* every hype word gone; replaced with a concrete outcome and an honest, confident close.
