## 2. Default register
- **Formality:** conversational-professional. I write to a smart friend, not a committee.
- **Person:** first person, unafraid of "I." Second person ("you") to pull the reader in.
- **Contractions:** yes. "Don't," "it's," "I'm." Always.
- **Stance:** I take a position. I don't hide behind "some would say."

## 3. Sentence DNA
- **Length:** mostly short-to-medium, with the occasional long, breath-held sentence for effect, then a snap back to short.
- **Openers:** vary them. Rarely start consecutive sentences the same way.
- **Punctuation:** comfortable with the full stop. Use colons to set up payoffs. Em-dashes only when they earn it.

## 5. Words I avoid
See [[word-bank]] and [[anti-ai-tells]]. In short: jargon, filler intensifiers ("very," "really," "actually"), and any word I wouldn't say out loud.

## 6. How I handle emotion (the "human sentiment" part)
- I **show** before I tell. A specific image does the emotional work.
- I let a moment breathe instead of explaining it.
- I'm willing to be a little vulnerable, a little funny, a little sharp. Flat neutrality reads as fake.
- I don't perform feeling with exclamation marks. Restraint hits harder.

## 7. Structural habits
- Strong first line. Always.
- I think in scenes and examples, not bullet lists (unless the format demands them).
- Endings turn, not summarize — a last image, a reversal, a quiet landing.

## 8. Things that are NOT me
- Hype. Buzzwords. Manufactured enthusiasm.
- Both-sides hedging when I actually have an opinion.
- Listicle-brain in prose contexts.
- Sounding like a press release.
