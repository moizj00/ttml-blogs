# Award-Winning Craft — The 6 Lenses & Severity Forbidden List

> Distilled from the [[Pulitzer Award-Winning Nonfiction Style Kit]] (built on 25 Pulitzer / National Magazine Award winners). This is the clean, agent-facing version — read this, not the raw transcript.
> Pairs with [[anti-ai-tells]] and the [[Pulitzer Award-Winning Nonfiction Style Kit]]. Load for any flagship piece.

## Core directive
Never produce generic AI content, summaries, or list-shaped thinking. Every flagship piece should feel like a high-agency opinion piece or cinematic narrative: earned through specific evidence and scenes, precise and vivid without purple prose, ending on a real payoff. **Delay abstraction until it's earned.** Build arguments as architecture, not hot takes. First person only as a thinking instrument.

## Step 0 — pick ONE lens (never mix structures)

| Lens | When | Hook | Structure (follow exactly) | Evidence mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Narrative Feature** | Story with human stakes, scenes, consequence | Scene / human stake first | Scene → stakes → context → complication → reported expansion → final meaning | Specific scenes, chronology, physical detail, interviews |
| **Personal Essay / Memoir** | Personal memory, identity, family, reflection | Concrete memory / confession / contradiction | Personal frame → scene sequence → reflection → broader human question → earned emotional turn | Memory, dialogue, precise objects, self-interrogation |
| **Argument / Cultural Analysis** | Making a case with history, policy, moral logic | Claim / contradiction / provocative question | Thesis → historical/cultural evidence → human example → counterpoint → sharpened conclusion | History, policy, close reading, lived example, contrast |
| **Investigative / Explanatory** | Explaining a system through one case + documents | Individual case exposing system failure | Case study → system explanation → documents/data → opposing view → consequences → reform question | Documents, records, triangulation, human impact |
| **Service / Explainer** | Reader problem → actionable steps with judgment | Reader's problem + immediate usefulness | Problem → why it matters → framework → steps/examples → reader action | Expert reporting + practical steps with judgment |
| **Literary Journalism** | Character portrait, telling detail as gateway | A telling detail as gateway | Detail → why it matters → observation/framework → insight/examples → deeper seeing | Precise detail, cultural/historical context |

> If the topic is ambiguous: generate 3 candidate lenses, score each against this table, pick the best, lock it. If hybrid, choose the primary and let the secondary only flavor it.

## The exemplars (north stars)
- **Narrative:** *Snow Fall* (John Branch) — cinematic scene, delayed context, restraint, meaning earned through consequence.
- **Argument:** *The Case for Reparations* (Ta-Nehisi Coates) — tight architecture; policy braided with one human life (Clyde Ross); moral clarity without preaching.
- **Personal:** *The Mother Who Changed* (Katie Engelhart) — concrete family scene and objects; self-interrogation; emotional turn without sentimentality.

## Severity-weighted forbidden list
See the full categorized list in [[anti-ai-tells]]. Triage:

- **Level 3 — instant rewrite:** Furthermore · Moreover · In addition · In conclusion · To summarize · The bottom line is · At the end of the day · In today's world · In the digital age · It is important to note · It's worth noting · Arguably · It seems that · The truth is · Experts agree.
- **Level 2 — strong penalty:** Heartbreaking · Profoundly moving · Deeply troubling · A tapestry of · Embark on a journey · Delve into · Dive deep into · Unlock the potential · At its core · This is a complex topic but… · This means that… · In other words… · Let's explore · The key takeaway is.
- **Level 1 — style deduction:** generic hedging, vague emotion, list-shaped thinking (unless Service lens requires it), meta-commentary.

**Golden rule:** if a sentence seems to *need* one of these to connect ideas, the structure or evidence is too weak — fix the architecture, not the transition.

## Output rules
1. Open with the lens's exact hook.
2. Follow the structure pattern; don't reorder or flatten.
3. Use only that lens's evidence mode — never "experts say" without a named source + context.
4. Restraint over melodrama: show emotion through concrete detail, never announce it.
5. End on an earned payoff — insight, sharpened question, moral resonance, or usable clarity. Never a formulaic closer.
6. After drafting, run the [[preflight-checklist]] + the Pulitzer pre-flight in the [[Pulitzer Award-Winning Nonfiction Style Kit|Style Kit]]; rewrite once if anything fails.
7. Output only the finished piece — no "Here's a draft," no visible process.

## Micro before/after
**Forbidden:** "In today's fast-paced world, it's important to note that climate change is a complex issue. Furthermore, experts agree we must act. In conclusion, we need to decarbonize."
**Award-grade (Service lens):** "The average U.S. household emits 8.4 tons of CO₂ from home energy alone. The 12-month sequence below cuts that by 37% while lowering the monthly bill — no lifestyle sacrifice required."
