# Anti-AI-Tells — The De-Robotizer

> The single highest-leverage file for "doesn't sound like AI." Scan every draft against this before delivering.

AI writing has a fingerprint. Readers feel it even when they can't name it. Here's the fingerprint — and how to remove it.

## A. Banned constructions (never produce)

- **The fake antithesis:** "It's not just X — it's Y." / "This isn't about X. It's about Y." Overused to death.
- **The hollow tricolon:** three balanced items in every other sentence — "faster, smarter, and more efficient."
- **Throat-clearing openers:** "In today's fast-paced world," "In the ever-evolving landscape of," "In the realm of," "Now more than ever."
- **The summary close:** "In conclusion," "Ultimately," "At the end of the day," "When all is said and done."
- **Self-narration:** "Let's dive in," "Buckle up," "Let's explore," "In this piece, I will."
- **Engagement-bait:** "Great question!", "You're absolutely right," "Here's the thing."
- **The rule-of-three obsession** applied to everything until the prose feels like a metronome.

## A2. Severity tiers (from the [[award-winning-craft|Award-Winning Craft]] kit)
Use this for fast triage on a draft. Higher tier = fix first.

- **Level 3 — instant rewrite:** Furthermore · Moreover · In addition · In conclusion · To summarize · The bottom line is · At the end of the day · In today's world · In the digital age · It is important to note · It's worth noting · Arguably · It seems that · The truth is · Experts agree (without a named source + context).
- **Level 2 — strong penalty:** Heartbreaking · Profoundly moving · Deeply troubling · A tapestry of · Embark on a journey · Delve into · Dive deep into · Unlock the potential · At its core · This is a complex topic but… · This means that… · In other words… · Let's explore · The key takeaway is · What this really shows is.
- **Level 1 — style deduction:** generic hedging, vague emotion, list-shaped thinking (outside the Service lens), meta-commentary, restating the request.

**Golden rule:** if a sentence seems to *need* one of these to connect ideas, the structure or evidence is too weak — fix the architecture, not the transition.

## B. Banned vocabulary (the LLM word-cloud)

delve, tapestry, testament, realm, landscape (figurative), navigate (figurative), unlock, unleash, elevate, leverage (verb), robust, seamless, streamline, game-changer, cutting-edge, boasts, nestled, bustling, vibrant, dive into, embark, journey (figurative), foster, harness, pivotal, crucial, vital, myriad, plethora, treasure trove, beacon, cornerstone, paramount, underscore, resonate (overused), holistic, synergy, ecosystem (non-tech), curated, meticulous, profound, profoundly, ever-evolving, fast-paced, in the world of.

> Not every word is forbidden forever — but if a draft is dense with these, it reads as machine output. Replace with plainer, truer words.

## C. Banned rhythms & textures

- **Uniform sentence length.** AI defaults to medium-length sentences forever. Break the pattern hard.
- **Every paragraph the same shape** (topic sentence → three supports → wrap). Vary paragraph architecture.
- **Em-dash as a verbal tic.** One or two per piece, deliberate. Not one per sentence.
- **Over-hedging:** "it's worth noting," "it could be argued," "generally speaking," "in many cases," "tends to," "somewhat," "arguably." Cut.
- **Over-signposting:** "Firstly... Secondly... Moreover... Furthermore... Additionally..." Use real transitions or none.
- **Hyper-balanced both-sidesism** when a real opinion is called for.
- **Emoji-as-punctuation** and exclamation inflation (unless the voice/format genuinely calls for it).

## D. The "soul" failures (why it feels hollow)

- **Announcing emotion instead of evoking it.** Replace "It was a deeply moving experience" with the concrete detail.
- **Generic praise / generic stakes.** "This is incredibly important" means nothing. Show why.
- **No risk.** AI plays it safe. Real writing has a point of view, a joke that might not land, a confession, a sharp edge.
- **Perfect symmetry.** Humans are a little lopsided. A great piece has one weird, specific, unbalanced thing in it.

## E. The 30-second scan (run before every delivery)
1. Search the draft for any word in section B. Replace or justify each.
2. Read the first sentence — does it restate the prompt or throat-clear? Rewrite.
3. Read the last sentence — does it say "in conclusion"? Rewrite.
4. Are 3+ consecutive sentences the same length? Break them.
5. Is any emotion *announced* rather than shown? Fix the strongest instance.
6. Is there one specific, surprising, human detail somewhere? If not, add it.

If it passes all six, it's probably free of the obvious tells.
